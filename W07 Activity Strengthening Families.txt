
1. What topic did you research?

I researched healing after a breakup and finding peace through Jesus Christ because I recently experienced a painful breakup with my wife and want to heal and move forward.

2. Summarize what you learned from the research.

I learned that Jesus Christ can give us peace and comfort during difficult times, with prayer, scripture study, and faith we can cope with emotional pain. I also learned that healing takes time, and I should be patient with myself as I recover.

Scripture: John 14:27 teaches us about the peace Jesus Christ offers. https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?lang=eng&id=p27#p27


3. What is the relationship with the person you discussed this family topic with?

I discussed this topic with my Sister, who shared advice and encouragement about dealing with heartbreak and finding peace.

4. What did you learn from your discussion?
Healing takes time, so I must be patient with myself.
Talking to someone I trust can help me cope with emotional pain.
Faith in Jesus Christ can give me strength and hope.
5. What will I START doing?

I will start praying more, reading scriptures, and taking better care of my emotional well-being.

6. What will I STOP doing?

I will stop blaming myself for everything and dwelling on painful memories that prevent me from moving forward.

7. What will I CONTINUE doing?

I will continue trusting God, working on myself, and taking small steps towards healing and happiness.
